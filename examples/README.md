# The best examples

Pages worth pointing a new team at, each with one line on why. All public; read on 16 November 2026.

## Token files

- [agenticcells.site/assets/style.css](https://agenticcells.site/assets/style.css): compact and measured; a ratio type scale, a phone menu with no JavaScript, tables that become cards on phones, diagrams styled by token classes, a decision id on each block. The starting point of our [pack](../pack/README.md).
- [promptugal.news/assets/tokens.css](https://promptugal.news/assets/tokens.css): every colour, face, size and space in one file, contrast ratios in the comments, five complete themes.
- [web-summit.sgit.ai/assets/site.css](https://web-summit.sgit.ai/assets/site.css): light and dark, status colours, each token citing the issue that added it, and a gate that fails on a colour outside the block.
- [The newsroom's themes and their README](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/assets/themes/README.md): how to add themes to a stylesheet you cannot edit, with the tests and the first bug.

## Brand guides

- [PrompTugal brand v2.0](https://promptugal.news/design/brand/Promptugal-Brand-Guide.pdf): ten pages that fit in one read: principles, wordmark, colour, type scale, clear space, voice.
- [riskmandate.ai brand page](https://riskmandate.ai/brand.html): the mark at three fidelities, clear space, minimum sizes and what never to do to it.

## Decisions and specs

- [The top of the page](https://promptugal.news/newsroom/design/decisions/the-top-of-the-page/index.md): the best decision page we have: four options on phone and desktop, does well and badly, reviewers, cost, recommendation, and the owner's answer recorded verbatim.
- [The design flip](https://promptugal.news/newsroom/design/decisions/the-design-flip/index.md): four rounds on one page, with corrections made in the open and a measured contrast table.
- [Spec for front page E](https://promptugal.news/board/spec-front-page-e.md): acceptance checks written before the build, then ticked against the live site.
- [riskmandate.ai design options](https://riskmandate.ai/design-options.html): a decision record that keeps the options that lost.
- [pt.newsroom directions](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/pt-newsroom/directions.html): four directions with motivation and trade-off; a design system written "so it can be built rather than imitated".

## Reviews

- [PrompTugal home-page review](https://promptugal.news/newsroom/design/home-review/index.md): verdict, what works, eight fixes in order each with an owner and a file, and what not to change.
- [web-summit site review](https://web-summit.sgit.ai/site-review/index.html): what was wrong, what changed, what is next, checked at 1440 and 390px.
- [Newsroom first-screen note](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/admin/notes/wires-ux/first-screen.md): pixel positions of each block before, a rule, then a spec at 1280 and 390.

## Design that serves a different job

- [riskmandate.ai console styles](https://riskmandate.ai/admin/console.css): an operator console designed for scanning and counting, not selling, with a rank scale where only "needs the lead" is filled.
- [web-summit home (WS-103)](https://web-summit.sgit.ai/design/index.html): a first screen that answers "what needs me, what is happening now, who is doing it" in under ten seconds.
- [Scrolled is not read](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/scrolled-is-not-read.html): a UX change grounded in measurement and prior art.

## Roles

- [sgit.ai designer role](https://sgit.ai/team/roles/designer.html) and [newsroom designer role](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/newsroom/roles/designer.html): what a designer agent owns, never does, and its definition of done (390px, `scrollWidth`, screenshots before and after).
