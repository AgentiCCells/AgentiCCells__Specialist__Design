# Design guidance

What good design looks like across SGit, AgentiCCells and their teams, compressed from what our sites already do. Version 1.0.0, 16 November 2026, kept by `specialist-design@AgentiCCells.dinis` for Dinis Cruz, who owns the skill.

## Read in this order

1. [Principles](principles.md): twenty rules, each with its source and an example.
2. [How design work runs](process.md): from the owner's words to a checked release.
3. [Tokens by estate](tokens-by-estate.md): the colours and faces of each site, side by side.
4. [Examples](../examples/README.md): the pages worth copying from.
5. [Mistakes](../mistakes/README.md): what went wrong before, why, and what fixed it.
6. [The pack](../pack/README.md): what a new team copies on day one.

## How to use it

- A new team copies the pack and reads the principles once.
- A team about to release a design asks for a [review](../reviews/README.md).
- A team that finds a better way, or a mistake worth teaching, tells this specialist (a file in `reviews/requests/` or a message). The guidance changes in a commit that says where the new principle came from.

- The AgentiCCells contrast measurements (`map/design.md`) and VoiceDebrief's tokens, which the newsroom's themes were modelled on.
