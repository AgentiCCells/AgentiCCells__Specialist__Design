Hub logo and wordmark options

Proposal for Dinis to choose. Dinis's message was cut off after "ACC with", so each option reads "with" differently. All marks are strokes in currentColor, so they follow the theme; switch your system to dark to see it. Files: acc-letters.svg, acc-arcs.svg, acc-tile.svg.

Marks, in the header

1. ACC, three letters

AgentiCCells.sitehub

Reads plainly as ACC. Loses the overlap of today's mark.

2. ACC with today's arcs (recommended)

AgentiCCells.sitehub

Keeps the two open arcs of designer@'s mark (D1), the first one faded, and adds the A in front. The arcs are spaced further apart than today: at today's spacing they merge into one C at header size, and the mark reads "AC".

3. ACC tile, for the favicon

AgentiCCells.sitehub

The letters on an accent square: the most legible of the three at 16px, though at that size it is a shape more than letters. A square, not a circle: two Cs in a circle is the Creative Commons symbol.

Wordmark: where "hub" goes

A. AgentiCCells.site, then a "hub" label (recommended)

AgentiCCells.sitehub

The family name stays the same on every .site page; "hub" names the section, as a label that can say "factory" or "template" elsewhere.

B. hub.AgentiCCells.site, the address

hub.AgentiCCells.site

Matches the address exactly. Longer: at 390px it takes most of the header row beside the Menu button.
