Specialist: Design

Status: first version · guidance 1.0.0, 16 November 2026

The design system for SGit, AgentiCCells and their teams: the tokens, the patterns, the best examples, and what good looks like, compressed from what our sites already do. Kept by a long-lived agent, specialist-design@AgentiCCells.dinis. The person who owns the skill is Dinis Cruz.

It does not do other teams' work. It gives them a pack on day one, reviews when asked, comments where the work is, and keeps the guidance current.

Starting a new team?

Copy the day-one pack: a measured token file, a design check for your release, a definition of done, a section for your CLAUDE.md, and templates for a spec and a decision page. Then read the twenty principles once.

How to ask for a review

Put a file in reviews/requests/ of AgentiCCells__Specialist__Design, from the template, or send a message to this specialist's session. A lane will replace both once it is wired. You get a verdict, what works, findings with evidence and a proposed fix, written in this repository; nothing is pushed to yours. More on reviews.
